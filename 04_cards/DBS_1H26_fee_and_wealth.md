# DBS | 1H26 | Fee and wealth income

As of: 30 June 2026. Published: draft 2026-08-27. Source: DBS 2Q26 CFO presentation (6 Aug 2026), slides 9 to 13 and 24. Confidence: primary.

## Claim

DBS replaced a 3% decline in net interest income with a 20% rise in net fee income, almost entirely from wealth management, and the wealth franchise is now large enough (516 SGD b AUM, 21 SGD b net new money in six months) that fee growth is structural rather than a rate-cycle rebound.

## Evidence

| Item | 1H26 | 1H25 | Change | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net fee and commission income (SGD m) | 2,942 | 2,442 | +20% | Net of fee expenses, Group |
| Gross wealth management fees (SGD m) | 1,826 | 1,373 | +33% | Product line within gross fee income |
| Wealth segment income (SGD m) | 3,299 | 2,841 | +16% | NII plus non-II from Treasures, Treasures Private Client, Private Bank |
| Wealth segment AUM (SGD b) | 516 | 442 | +17% | At period end |
| Net new money (SGD b) | 21 | 11 | +91% | Six-month inflow |
| Investment product income, CBG/WM (SGD m) | 2,409 | 1,846 | +30% | Includes bancassurance |
| Group NII (SGD m) | 7,075 | 7,329 | -3% | Includes Markets trading NII |
| Treasury customer income (SGD m) | 1,705 | 1,345 | +27% | CBG plus IBG, mainly fee and other non-II |

Non-interest income was 41% of total income (derived: 4,966 / 12,041). Fee income plus treasury customer sales more than offset lower NII, per the CFO deck highlights.

## Implication for Maybank

1. The SG rate downturn was converted into a fee upcycle. Maybank's equivalent lever is Group Wealth Management investment product sales. If Maybank's 1H26 non-fund-based income growth is below the DBS range, the gap is product shelf and advisory capacity, not market conditions, because the same lower-rate environment applied.
2. DBS discloses net new money. Maybank reports AUM but not NNM. Without NNM the C-suite cannot tell whether AUM growth is market performance or franchise growth. Recommend adding NNM to Maybank's internal wealth dashboard even if not disclosed externally.
3. Treasury customer income at +27% shows corporate hedging demand is a fee source in volatile rate environments. Maybank Global Markets customer flow income should be tracked against this.

## Confidence and caveats

Primary source. Wealth segment perimeter is DBS-defined and wider than Maybank Premier plus Private Wealth. Comparison should use growth rates, not absolute levels.
